# Desktop 0.4

The office renders each active team member as a clickable seated person. Idle computers are off. Rooms on the right reserve space for collaboration and review. The scene supports working and meeting activity inputs, including arrival and conversation animation, but production has no hosted activity feed yet; imported historical transcripts do not activate people or meetings.

Office and Tasks show the same all-project queue, grouping each investigation and its contract review requests into one card. New request atomically creates the research details and its blocked queue entry. Research edits those details; it does not provide a separate experiment-creation flow. Cancel request cancels open linked requests and makes the research details read-only. A new investigation requires an explicit New request.

Agents lists all members and allows removal/restoration. Removal retains identity, profile and conversation history for attribution. Click a person or Profile & logs to edit name, team, role, instructions or effort. Provider/model identity remains tied to the authenticated connection.

Subscription authentication and usage reads remain local metadata operations. No paid API fallback, local research execution, or Colab integration was added.
