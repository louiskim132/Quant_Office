# w5 — Comparator study: Slack + Discord (presence/notifications, queue/status UX)

Round `ui-benchmark-1`, worker-5, base `65bddec303aadd95fb21585c7a48a8685bf3c8d8`.
Comparators: **Slack** (slack.com help center + marketing pages) and **Discord** (support.discord.com + discord.com). Assigned focus: presence and notifications (presence dots, badges, unread models, notification center) vs the office's bell + NEEDS YOU panel + "N need you" status bar, and queue/status UX (channel lists vs the office nav/request rows).

## 1. Scope and capture method

All captures used **method (a)**: direct Playwright capture (script `swipe/worker-5/capture.cjs`, `capture2.cjs`) of public help/marketing pages — full-page screenshot `.png`, rendered HTML `.html`, text extract `.txt` (with URL/final-URL/title/HTTP/timestamp headers), plus embedded article images downloaded as `*-imgN.*` (real product UI screenshots where docs included them). Machine index: `swipe/worker-5/manifest.json`.

- Slack help center article IDs were verified through Slack's own help-center search (`swipe/worker-5/discover.cjs`); Discord article IDs were verified through web search, then captured live (HTTP 200 each).
- Slack's web client and Discord's app are behind sign-in, so product-UI evidence comes from the screenshots embedded in the vendors' own support articles (e.g. `swipe/worker-5/discord-inbox-faq-img0.png` is a real Discord client capture published by Discord). Limitation is documented in section 6.
- Methods (b) MCP and (c) pattern galleries were not needed; nothing was purchased (d).

## 5. Proposed revision packet seeds (ranked by expected impact)

1. **Promote NEEDS YOU from dropdown to a triage surface.** Problem: the bell popover (`live/f-11-attention-panel.png`) is a flat list; items can't be filtered, dismissed individually, or bulk-cleared — unlike Slack Activity's per-type filters + clear (`swipe/worker-5/slack-help-activity-view.txt`) and Discord Inbox's Mentions/Unreads tabs + Mark-Inbox-As-Read (`swipe/worker-5/discord-inbox-faq-img1.png`). Proposal: keep the bell, but make the panel (or a page it deep-links to) a real triage surface — item kind icon, deep link, per-item acknowledge, "mark all handled". Rough scope: `app/src/renderer/shell-widgets.tsx` (AttentionBell/bell-pop), `app/src/shared/attention.ts` (read), possibly a small new view; statusbar counter wiring in `main.tsx`.
2. **Grade attention levels: actionable vs informational.** Problem: the office has one badge number for everything that needs you, while Slack separates bold-name unread vs numbered mention badge (`swipe/worker-5/slack-help-notifications.txt`) and Discord separates gray unread vs red mention counts (`swipe/worker-5/discord-text-channels.txt`, `discord-channel-mute.txt`). Proposal: keep `needs you` as the actionable class and add a quieter informational class (e.g. recently-failed, finished-since-visit) with distinct weight — possibly a statusbar-only or sidebar-dot treatment instead of a red badge. Rough scope: `main.tsx` nav badge + statusbar counts, `shell-widgets.tsx`, `status.ts` vocabulary.
3. **Unify agent presence semantics on the topbar chips.** Problem: agent state is honest but scattered — colored rings on chips (`live/live-20-settled-office.png`), "Working · 29s" bubble in the scene, pills in Agents table (`live/live-10-running-office.png`). Slack's single availability-dot + status-emoji model (`swipe/worker-5/slack-help-status.txt`) and Discord's status picker (`swipe/worker-5/discord-status-img0.png`) show the value of one consistent glyph per entity. Proposal: give each topbar chip the shared `status.ts` icon + label (never colour alone), plus a tooltip naming provenance ("provider-reported"). Rough scope: `main.tsx` chip rendering, `status.ts` mapping.
4. **"New since you last looked" marker on request rows.** Problem: nothing in the queue says what changed since the user's last visit — Discord's red NEW divider (`swipe/worker-5/discord-text-channels-img0.png`) and Slack's Unreads sort (`swipe/worker-5/slack-help-unreads-view.txt`) both solve re-orientation. Proposal: a first-unseen divider or "new" dot per request row. Rough scope: `queue.tsx`, `projects.tsx` request rows + a persisted last-seen marker.
5. **Keyboard triage path.** Problem: Slack dedicates Ctrl+Shift+A to Unreads and Alt+↑/↓ to conversation nav (`swipe/worker-5/slack-help-keyboard-nav.txt`); the office has a command palette (`populated-command-palette.png` in the baseline set) but no documented triage shortcut. Proposal: shortcut to focus NEEDS YOU, j/k item nav, Enter to deep-link. Rough scope: `main.tsx` keybindings + `shell-widgets.tsx`.
6. **Quiet-hours / focus mode for the office shell.** Problem: Slack's pause-notifications + schedule (`swipe/worker-5/slack-help-pause-notifs.txt`) and Discord's DND (`swipe/worker-5/discord-status.txt`) suppress ambient alerting; the office flashes the taskbar on every need-you transition (`main.tsx` ~line 695) with no throttle. Proposal: user-settable quiet hours or a "working session" toggle that suppresses taskbar flash while keeping the panel updated. Rough scope: `main.tsx` badge/flash block + Settings.

## 6. Gaps and honesty notes

- **No signed-in capture**: both products gate their real clients behind accounts. Product-UI evidence is the vendors' own published screenshots embedded in help articles (e.g. `swipe/worker-5/discord-inbox-faq-img0.png`, `discord-status-img0.png`) — authentic but not current-build and chosen by the vendor.
- **Slack help imagery is stylized**: all article images resolved to one shared illustration (`slack-help-activity-work-img2.png`, kept once after dedupe) — a profile card with the green availability dot — not literal UI screenshots. Slack behavioral claims therefore rest on article *text* (captured in `.txt` extracts), which is authoritative documentation but not pixel evidence. Discord's Zendesk attachments, by contrast, are literal client screenshots (dated ~2020–2024 UI).
- **Failed captures removed**: five guessed Slack IDs and four guessed Discord IDs 404'd or resolved to wrong articles; they are recorded in `manifest.json` under `failedAttempts` and their files deleted, so every cited artifact exists.
- **Discord marketing captured in ko-KR** (`discord-home.txt`): geo/locale redirect; not analyzed for content.
- **Discord Inbox is desktop/browser-only** per the FAQ (`discord-inbox-faq.txt`) — relevant if the office ever scopes a mobile surface.
- Methods (b) and (c) unused; nothing purchased (d). Scores are my judgment of the captured evidence, not verified against live product builds.
